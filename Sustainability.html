Growth with a limit

Tourism that protects what it sells.

The Sundarbans, the tea highlands, the rivers — the entire brand depends on them staying intact. Growth is designed around that limit, not against it.

Commitments

Three working principles.

Community

Local-first economics

Homestays, community-run boat cooperatives, and local guide licensing keep tourism revenue inside the regions that host it, rather than routing it through outside operators.

Capacity

Visitor limits in sensitive zones

The Sundarbans and hill-tract regions operate under permit and capacity systems, capping daily visitor numbers to protect wildlife corridors and fragile trails.

Infrastructure

Low-impact by design

New lodges and transit points are built to local vernacular standards — timber and brick over concrete, designed to blend into riverbank and hill settings.
