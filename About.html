The initiative

A top South Asian destination, by 2035.

A national vision to close the gap between what Bangladesh offers and what the world knows about it — without losing what makes it worth visiting.

"To position Bangladesh as a globally recognized destination for authentic culture, nature, and heritage tourism — driven by sustainable growth, local empowerment, and world-class visitor experience."

Why now

The awareness gap is the strategy.

The problem

Bangladesh has world-class rivers, tea highlands, and heritage sites, but almost no presence in global travel conversation — outranked by neighbors with a fraction of its natural and cultural depth.

The opening

The traveler this initiative targets — 25–40, culture and adventure-led, active on Instagram and YouTube — is actively searching for exactly what mass destinations no longer offer: the undiscovered.

Five pillars

How the vision gets built.

01 Brand

Global awareness & branding

A consistent destination identity across social, agency, and press channels abroad.

02 Community

Sustainable, community-based tourism

Growth structured to pay local operators first, not last.

03 Access

Improved infrastructure & safety

Transit, signage, and visitor services built to international standards.

04 Digital

Digital-first travel experience

Trip planning, routing, and booking designed for how modern travelers actually research.

05 Preserve

Preserve heritage and nature

Protecting the rivers, forests, and sites the entire vision is built on.

2035 is a decade of building.
It starts with the first visit.
