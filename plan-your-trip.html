Logistics

Everything before the flight.

Visas, seasons, budget, and getting around — the practical layer under every route on this site.

Visa

Entry requirements

Most Western and SE Asian nationals can apply for an e-visa or visa-on-arrival. Apply 2–4 weeks ahead of travel; processing is typically 3–7 business days.

Season

When to go

November–February: dry, cool, best overall. March–May: hot, pre-monsoon. June–October: monsoon — rivers full, landscapes green, fewer travelers.

Budget

Daily cost

Independent budget travel runs $25–40/day. Mid-range with guided river or forest excursions runs $60–100/day, tea-garden lodges and boat charters included.

Transit

Getting around

Rail connects Dhaka to Sylhet and the north efficiently. River ferries reach the Sundarbans and delta towns. Hill regions require jeep transfer.

Good to know

Travel with intent.

Book local

Use community-based operators

Homestays and local boat operators keep tourism income inside the villages you visit.

Pack light

Modest, breathable clothing

Bangladesh is a majority-Muslim country; light, covering clothing is comfortable and respectful outside beach areas.

Go slow

Fewer stops, more days

Rivers and rural routes reward a slower pace — plan 2+ nights per region rather than day-tripping.
